PRIVACY.

Project 61 collects only the information needed to understand and respond to project inquiries. The site does not currently offer user accounts, shopping carts or payments.

What we collect

If you send a project brief, we request your name, email, selected service, project description, optional organization and estimate information, and any links you choose to provide.

How inquiries are processed

After form submission, the data is validated and delivered as an email through Resend to the Project 61 inquiry inbox. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to limit automated spam. Vercel hosts the website and handles the server endpoint. Those providers may process technical request information under their own terms.

Retention and sharing

Project inquiry emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the requested conversation and meet applicable obligations. We do not sell inquiry information or publish submitted briefs. We do not currently maintain a separate customer database through this website.

Tracking and cookies

We have not added advertising trackers or a separate analytics platform. Cloudflare Turnstile may process browser and security information as part of bot prevention. External sites linked from our portfolio have their own privacy practices.

Requests or corrections

To ask about or request deletion of your submitted inquiry information, contact anticipate.peak@gmail.com. This is our interim inbox pending a Project 61 domain and email address.
